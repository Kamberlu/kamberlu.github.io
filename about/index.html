ABOUT

WHO I AM

I'm Kamber Lucas — a sustainability consultant. I spent two-plus years in Deloitte's sustainability practice delivering greenhouse-gas inventories, decarbonization strategies, and climate-reporting engagements (CSRD, TCFD, GHG Protocol) for Fortune 500 and institutional clients, and I now consult independently on market research, financial modeling, and management strategy. I hold an MBA in Corporate Sustainability with a graduate certificate in Carbon Management from Colorado State University, and a BS in Management with a statistics minor from the University of Wyoming.

Before the business side: I grew up in Alaska and spent three summers running a remote, off-grid salmon sonar site on the Nushagak River for the Alaska Department of Fish & Game — producing the data that helps sustainably manage the world's largest wild sockeye salmon fishery. I served as a Peace Corps Volunteer in North Macedonia and continue remote service today, currently helping a Ukrainian municipal government pursue infrastructure funding.

The throughline: I've worked both sides of this site's argument — hands-on with the living systems in the atlases, and professionally inside the capital flows and corporate reporting that decide their fate.

WHAT THIS PROJECT IS

Conscious Cash is a personal, independent project with one thesis: the climate and nature crises are, underneath everything, capital allocation problems — and solvable ones. The site makes that case in four data-driven pages: the money, the emissions it buys, the living world it costs, and what consciously redirected capital is already building.

Everything is hand-coded, every figure traces to a public source cited on its page, and projections are always labelled as projections. Optimism here is earned, not assumed. Data reflects the latest releases as of August 2026; I update the pages as the major sources publish. Corrections and suggestions are welcome.

This site reflects my own views and is not affiliated with any organisation named on it. Nothing here is investment advice.
